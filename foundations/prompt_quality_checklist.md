# Prompt Quality Checklist

Use this checklist to review and improve prompts.

## Checklist

* Is the task clearly defined?
* Is the target audience specified?
* Is the desired output format included?
* Are constraints clearly stated?
* Is important context provided?
* Are safety requirements included?
* Does the prompt avoid ambiguity?
* Does the prompt prevent hallucinated details?
* Is the expected tone clear?
* Can the response be evaluated objectively?

## Example

Weak prompt:

Explain ventilators.

Improved prompt:

Explain mechanical ventilation to a first-year nursing student.

Include:

* What it is
* Why it is used
* Basic risks
* Important monitoring points

Use simple language and clear headings.
